During the siege of Nariman House in November 2008, a voice on the phone from Pakistan told two gunmen to pile mattresses at the top of the stairs and wait for the commandos. File 05 of The Sisodia Files follows Sajid Mir — the Lashkar-e-Taiba handler the FBI calls the chief planner of 26/11: the quiet recruiter, the 2005 scouting trip, the Karachi control room, the years Pakistan let the world believe he was dead, the closed-door 2022 conviction timed to a FATF review, and China's holds on his UN listing.

Built from the FBI's wanted notice, David Headley's testimony in the 2011 Chicago trial, the Jamestown Foundation's analysis of Mir's conviction, and reporting by Dawn, Al Jazeera, ProPublica and PBS Frontline. Unconfirmed reports — including the December 2023 poisoning claims — are labelled as such.
