# RAW FILES — EP05: N.F. SUNTOOK — Research Dossier

Compiled 2026-09-30 for The Sisodia Files. POV: pro-India; Indian sources first; India's account first where contested.

## 0. READ THIS FIRST — research quality and limits

- **Material on Suntook himself is THIN.** There is no biography, no interview, no memoir, no obituary, no photograph and no death date that I could find. Almost everything rests on THREE Indian text sources: (a) B. Raman's 2004 column "On The RAW Hunt" (ORF/Outlook), (b) Shashank Mattoo's X thread of 14 Jan 2025 (which itself cites Brewster/Rai, Raman, The Hindu, The Diplomat), (c) short lines in Yatish Yadav's book (as quoted by a Bengali book blog) and DailyO. Everything else (Wikipedia etc.) is derivative.
- **Do not build the episode as a biography. Build it around the 1977-80 survival story** (Section 9 and 10 have the structure), with the 1983 Mauritius / "CIA mole" episode as the payoff. That is where the sourced drama is.
- **Tool limits:** WebFetch summarises pages with a small model, so all "verbatim" quotes below came through that layer and MUST be re-checked against the original page/book before they go on screen. The WebSearch budget for the session ran out (~30 searches done), so several primary items could not be opened (see Section 11 "Could not open"). Kaoboys of R&AW and Yadav's book full text were not accessible legally (borrow-only / Cloudflare-blocked); I did not pull pirated copies.
- **Confidence tags used below:** [2+] = two or more independent-looking sources; [1] = single source (flagged); [WIKI] = Wikipedia only.

## 1. Core facts (dated timeline)

- **Full name:** Naushervan (also "Nowsher") Framji Suntook, a Parsi (Gujarati-speaking per a Raman-derived note). Name spelling per Mattoo thread: "Naushervan Framji Suntook". [1: https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1879140684822901001.html] Gujarati-speaking Parsi: Wikipedia RAW page (derivative). Birth date, death date: NOT FOUND.
- **Career before RAW:** Indian Navy, then Indian Police Service, then Indian Frontier Administrative Service (IFAS), worked in the Northeast, helped train intelligence agencies in Africa. Source: Mattoo thread (tweet 8) https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1879140684822901001.html ; the "Navy -> Police -> IFAS" sequence also in Aviation Defence Universe "India's RAW turns 50" https://www.aviation-defence-universe.com/indias-raw-turns-50/ [2, but both are secondary]. NOTE: the ADU/Mattoo text says he served in the "Indian Police Service"; Raman and DailyO describe him only as IFAS. Your brief says "IFAS, not IPS" — the IFAS-not-police-cadre framing is the one Indian commentators use (DailyO: "accidental chief"). Flag the IPS line as contradictory/[1].
- **IFAS** = cadre created 1954 to administer NEFA (today's Arunachal Pradesh) — described in a WION opinion piece and a Claude Arpi paper (Arpi PDF could not be read). https://www.wionews.com/opinions-blogs/why-the-indian-frontier-administrative-service-could-be-a-potent-force-against-china-311089
- **1968 (Sept):** RAW created under R.N. Kao. (Raman: joined "R&AW when it was created in September 1968".) https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Kaoboys_of_R_AW_Down_Memory_Lane.html?id=nsBerwgsf38C
- **Pre-1977 (date unknown):** Suntook is "Founder Director of RRC (Radio Research Centre)" per the Wikipedia succession table. [WIKI only] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_of_the_Research_and_Analysis_Wing
- **24 Mar 1977:** Morarji Desai sworn in as PM (Janata government; runs to 15 Jul 1979). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premiership_of_Morarji_Desai
- **Apr 1977:** Desai's government makes Kao step down (DailyO: Kao "ordered to step down"; Wikipedia: Kao "resigned quietly ... A thorough inquiry cleared him and the R&AW of all blame"). https://www.dailyo.in/politics/k-sankaran-nair-r-and-aw-head-kashmir-emergency-rajiv-gandhi-asian-games-7438 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._N._Kao
- **1977 (about three months):** K. Sankaran Nair heads RAW; Desai orders staff cut to two-thirds, halt to operations in neighbouring countries, end to help for Chakma refugees; Nair resists, then asks to be moved rather than preside over the downgrade of the chief's post. DailyO (Nair obituary). Nair later retired/moved to the Minorities Commission (Dec 1978 per DailyO). https://www.dailyo.in/politics/k-sankaran-nair-r-and-aw-head-kashmir-emergency-rajiv-gandhi-asian-games-7438
- **1977:** Post of Secretary (Research) downgraded to "Director, R&AW" (lower rank than Cabinet Secretary-level). Suntook appointed at a rank below Kao's. DailyO 2016: "Suntook's appointment at a rank inferior to Kao's" https://www.dailyo.in/politics/raw-rn-kao-indira-gandhi-26-11-ak-verma-rajinder-khanna-ib-13054 ; Wikipedia Nair/Secretary pages (derivative). Secretary designation only restored in 1986 under Rajiv Gandhi (Wikipedia, derivative).
- **1977-79:** Desai cuts RAW: staff strength reduced, big cross-border operations cancelled, Information Division-type sections shut (ADU); budget cut ~30% (Raman via secondary summaries; see caveat). https://www.aviation-defence-universe.com/indias-raw-turns-50/
- **1977-78:** Janata ministers (Charan Singh as Home Minister) review files; government states in Parliament RAW was not guilty in the allegation about payment to the Shah of Iran's sister; Charan Singh tells Kao the campaign allegations were wrong (Raman). https://www.orfonline.org/research/on-the-raw-hunt (search-snippet paraphrase; re-verify on page)
- **1978 (alleged, contested):** Desai's phone remark to Zia about Kahuta — see Section 8.
- **28 Jul 1979 - 14 Jan 1980:** Charan Singh PM (Wikipedia); Janata collapses. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premiership_of_Morarji_Desai (mentions Charan Singh succession)
- **Jan 1980:** Indira Gandhi returns to power; Kao recalled as her security adviser (Wikipedia Kao: "He returned when Indira made a comeback in 1980"). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._N._Kao
- **Feb-Mar 1983:** Mauritius crisis: Jugnauth in Delhi seeking help vs feared Berenger coup; Operation Lal Dora contingency planned (Army/Navy). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Lal_Dora
- **Mar 1983:** MMM (Berenger) leaves Jugnauth's cabinet; **Apr 1983:** Jugnauth forms the Militant Socialist Movement (MSM). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anerood_Jugnauth
- **Late Mar 1983:** Day before his 31 March retirement, Indira Gandhi asks Suntook to go on a "sensitive mission" to a friendly foreign country; service extended a few days, farewell party postponed (Raman). https://www.orfonline.org/research/on-the-raw-hunt
- **During the mission:** a disgruntled subordinate plants a false story with a Telegraph (Calcutta) correspondent that IB had found Suntook was a CIA mole who fled to the USA via Kathmandu with top-secret files; Telegraph splashes it; other papers follow; Home Minister P.C. Sethi has to clarify in Parliament that he is abroad on assignment (Mattoo tweet 14; Sethi's statement [1]). https://www.orfonline.org/research/on-the-raw-hunt ; https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1879140684822901001.html
- **7 Apr 1983:** Suntook returns; **8 Apr 1983:** retires normally; farewell held as planned (Raman, via fetch summary: "returned ... on April 7 and retired normally the next day"). https://www.orfonline.org/research/on-the-raw-hunt  **So the "1977-1983" tenure effectively ends 8 April 1983.**
- **Mid-1983:** Jugnauth forms MSM-led coalition; **21 or 23 Aug 1983** election (Wikipedia election page says 21 Aug; Jugnauth page says 23 Aug — DISCREPANCY, check Mauritian electoral records): MSM-Labour-PMSD alliance wins 46 seats to MMM's 22. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_Mauritian_general_election
- **1983:** Girish Chandra Saxena (IPS) succeeds as RAW chief (1983-86). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girish_Chandra_Saxena
- **After 1983:** two years on the Minorities Commission; retires to a small house in Gurgaon; **wife died 1991; health deteriorates 1993**; a newspaper runs a sympathetic "distinguished former chief, living alone, uncared for" piece (Raman). https://www.orfonline.org/research/on-the-raw-hunt
- **Death:** date NOT FOUND. Raman's 2004 text calls him "the late N.F. Suntook", so he died before June 2004 [inference].

**Tenure length:** ~6 years (Mattoo: "after six years as RAW chief"). Wikipedia says 1977-1983 [2]. Mattoo's tweet calls him "longest-serving" in one search snippet source (Parsi Khabar/aggregated) — unverified, do not use.

## 2. The defining contribution

**Defining contribution = SURVIVAL: keeping RAW intact through the hostile Janata years (1977-80) so Indira Gandhi and Kao could inherit a working agency in 1980.**

Specifics available:
- The setup: Desai had called RAW Indira's "praetorian guard" before the election and promised to wind it up; in office he did not (Raman: "He did not."), but he cut budget (~30%), staff (to roughly two-thirds), cancelled cross-border operations, forced out Kao and then Nair, and downgraded the chief's rank. (Raman via search summaries; DailyO 2016; DailyO Nair obituary; bharatdocs.) 
- Yatish Yadav's verdict (via songramernotebook excerpt page): Desai "put N.F. Suntook in charge of the agency and somehow, he saved R&AW from being plundered by its own customer—the government." https://songramernotebook.com/archives/57247 [1 - re-check in book]
- DailyO (2016) labels him "an accidental chief" who was not a police officer in an agency dominated by police cadres. https://www.dailyo.in/politics/raw-rn-kao-indira-gandhi-26-11-ak-verma-rajinder-khanna-ib-13054
- Desai-era DailyO Nair piece describes Suntook as "a pliable officer" — the negative framing. India-first account: pliable or shrewd, RAW survived. Present both in one line.
- **Secondary contribution:** 1983 Mauritius. Working with High Commissioner Prem Singh, Suntook approached Hindu and Muslim leaders of Indian descent to back Jugnauth in a moderate Hindu-led coalition; Jugnauth won fresh elections; "pro-India, pro-West" policy followed; Brewster says India likely funded MSM (Mattoo tweets 10-11, citing Brewster/Rai "Operation Lal Dora"). https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1879140684822901001.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Lal_Dora
- **Institutional legacy (WEAK, verify before use):** Wikipedia lists him as "Founder Director of RRC (Radio Research Centre), ETS" [WIKI only]. The Wikipedia ETS page says ETS was "Established in the mid-1980s under the then R&AW chief N. F. Suntook" — this CONTRADICTS itself, since Suntook left in April 1983. ADU says RRC and ETS were "added ... in the 1970s and 1990s" (vague). Wikipedia's two supporting sources (SSPC "Strengthening India's External Intelligence Infrastructure", defenceforumindia) are weak. **Recommendation: do NOT state that Suntook founded RAW's SIGINT arm as fact. At most: "listed in some accounts as founder-director of the Radio Research Centre".** Also: there is a Raman line (from search snippet only) crediting "Kao, Suntook and Saxena" with keeping the CIA liaison going while watching CIA/ISI in Afghanistan — [1], unverified.
- "Helped train African intelligence services": only Mattoo tweet 8 [1, no underlying source]. Flag.

## 3. Cold-open candidates

**A. "The spy who defected" (best).** Late March 1983. The Telegraph, Calcutta, front page: the chief of RAW is a CIA mole, fled via Kathmandu with a suitcase of top-secret files. Parliament erupts; Home Minister P.C. Sethi must stand up and say the man is abroad on assignment. In truth, on Indira Gandhi's personal request the night before his retirement, Suntook is in Mauritius, stitching together an Indian-descent coalition to save an ally. He flies home 7 April, retires 8 April, farewell party held. Sources: Raman https://www.orfonline.org/research/on-the-raw-hunt ; Mattoo https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1879140684822901001.html
  - Twist for narration: the rumour was planted by a disgruntled RAW subordinate he had disciplined (Raman). Present as Raman's claim [1].
**B. "The postponed farewell party."** The eve of 31 March 1983: a farewell party is arranged; Indira Gandhi calls him in and asks for one more mission; party postponed; his service extended "by a few days". Small, human, sourced (Raman). Good opener if you want quiet rather than shock.
**C. "The man who inherits a wrecked house" (Apr 1977).** Kao out, Nair out after three months for refusing to cut staff to two-thirds, and the government appoints a man at a lower rank. The camera is on the empty chair. Sourced: DailyO Nair obituary; DailyO 2016. Framed as India's account; use the "pliable" line as the counter.
  - Rejected as opener: the Desai-Zia phone call (too contested, see Section 8) — better used in the middle.

## 8. Contested points / caveats (India's account first)

1. **Desai and Kahuta (India's mainstream account first):** B. Raman (2007, Kaoboys) wrote that in an "indiscreet moment" Desai told Zia that India knew of Kahuta; popularised by DailyO (24 Jun 2015) and Swarajya (Oct 2015): RAW had a source ready to deliver Kahuta blueprints for about $10,000; Desai refused to pay ("wrong to meddle in the internal affairs of a neighbouring state"); after the call, RAW's Kahuta assets were rolled up. https://www.dailyo.in/politics/morarji-desai-kargil-war-pervez-musharraf-pakistan-raw-kahuta-nuclear-warfare-3802 ; https://swarajyamag.com/magazine/how-we-killed-the-kaoboys
   - **Counter-claim / weakness (one line):** ThePrint (Apoorva Mandhani, 23 May 2025) notes Raman's book "does not speak of" agents being killed, and Desai's descendant Madhukeshwar Desai calls the charge "baseless" with no formal inquiry backing it. https://theprint.in/politics/story-behind-treason-charge-against-morarji-desai-why-congress-is-reviving-it-to-target-jaishankar/2634867/ ; bharatdocs (weak/unknown site) says the RAW weakening is documented but the agent-killings are not. https://bharatdocs.com/morarji-desai-kahuta-raw-controversy/ 
   - **How to script it:** "India's own former officers say ...; Desai's family denies it; no document has surfaced." NEVER state the agent killings as fact. Note the claim is politically weaponised in 2025 (Congress used it against Jaishankar). One-line neutrality is enough.
   - The Swarajya/DailyO "hair from a barber shop" Kahuta discovery story is a well-known Indian folklore item [1-2 popular sources]; not verified independently.
   - The Israel-strike/"refuelling denied" claim (Sunil Kumar personal blog) is unverified opinion; do not use.
2. **Desai's motive:** Raman thought he had been manipulated by flattery; the alternative reading is Gandhian truthfulness with neighbours (DailyO). Also, per Raman, Desai had called RAW Indira's "praetorian guard", but on reading the files "realised that these allegations were not true" (Raman). Charan Singh's generosity to Kao is a redeeming detail — a useful nuance: the Janata leadership corrected itself.
3. **Budget cut figure:** "30%" (Raman/Sunil Kumar) vs "staff to two-thirds" (DailyO Nair piece / bharatdocs). Different metrics; do not merge. "Some sources cite two-thirds reduction" of budget (search summary) is unverified.
4. **Suntook's cadre:** IFAS (Raman/DailyO) vs "Navy, IPS, then IFAS" (Mattoo/ADU). Also "helped train African intelligence agencies" and "Radio Research Centre/ETS founder" are single-source [1]/[WIKI]. ETS founded "mid-1980s under Suntook" is internally impossible (he retired 1983).
5. **"Pliable officer"** (DailyO on Nair's exit) vs "saved RAW" (Yadav). Both exist. India-first account: RAW survived under him.
6. **The CIA-mole story:** India's account: baseless, planted by a disgruntled subordinate; no evidence; Press Council complaint; Raman's angle is about media irresponsibility. There is no counter-claim in Indian sources; I found nothing supporting it. A subtlety: the "leaker" is Raman's account [1]; Mattoo says "some RAW officers" [1].
7. **Lal Dora:** India's account: a contingency plan to protect an ally against a feared coup. Counter/nuance: Brewster says the Army and Navy could not agree who would lead; the operation was never executed; Brewster says MSM likely got Indian money; the "coup" may have been imagined ("imagined MMM-led coup" per an allAfrica/L'Express summary). One line: "Mauritian and foreign scholars call it interference; Delhi called it protecting an ally and the Indian community." Wikipedia says Lal Dora was "executed" by Suntook (wrong: it was not executed as a military op; his mission was the intelligence-led political effort).
8. **Election date:** 21 Aug vs 23 Aug 1983 (two Wikipedia pages disagree).
9. **Ravindra Kaushik ("Black Tiger")** was exposed in 1983 during his tenure (Wikipedia: captured 1983). Different sources link it to Desai-era indiscretion or to a later junior operative's capture. Not Suntook-specific; do NOT tie it to Suntook without a source. I kept it out.

## 9. Why the public barely knows the name

- **Suntook took no public credit:** no memoir, no interviews found; he kept a low profile, retired to a small Gurgaon house (Raman); widowed 1991; ill by 1993.
- **He was an "accidental chief":** not IPS in a police-dominated agency; appointed at a lower rank by a government that wanted RAW smaller (DailyO).
- **His success was a non-event:** the best possible outcome of 1977-80 was that nothing was destroyed. No operation to point to; no book-friendly triumph (Bangladesh under Kao, Sikkim under Kao).
- **His Mauritius mission was secret** ("never publicly disclosed" per the Lal Dora account), and his one moment of public notice (April 1983) was a smear. The press that called him a CIA mole in April 1983 had no correction to run.
- **Kao's shadow:** Kao is the mythologised founder; Suntook sits between Kao (1968-77) and Saxena (1983-86, Blue Star/Kanishka).
- **Historiography:** the Janata years are usually told as "Desai wrecked RAW"; Suntook is a footnote in that villain story.
- **Only ~3 Indian authors wrote about him** (Raman, Yadav, Mattoo). Wikipedia has no Suntook article (the URL 404s).
