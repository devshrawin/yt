## Description (5702/5000)
```
In 1955 a bomb brought down the Kashmir Princess, an airliner carrying delegates of China's Premier Zhou Enlai. Nehru sent one Intelligence Bureau officer to find out who did it. Episode 1 of RAW Files follows that officer — Rameshwar Nath Kao — as he builds India's Research and Analysis Wing from about 250 people in 1968, warns of Pakistan's war plans in January 1971, backs the Mukti Bahini, and is asked by Indira Gandhi about Sikkim: "Can you do something about this?"

Built from Nitin Gokhale's biography "R.N. Kao: Gentleman Spymaster", B. Raman's "The Kaoboys of R&AW", and reporting in ThePrint, The Tribune, Swarajya and DailyO. Claims that rest on a single source are marked "reportedly" in the narration.
```
