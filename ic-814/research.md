## The hijacking (Wikipedia, "Indian Airlines Flight 814")
- Airbus A300B2-101, VT-EDW, Kathmandu (Tribhuvan) → Delhi, 24 Dec 1999. 190 aboard: 179 passengers, 11 crew.
- Hijack began 16:53 IST in Indian airspace. Five Harkat-ul-Mujahideen members, Pakistani nationals
  (Ibrahim Athar, Shahid Akhtar Sayeed, Sunny Ahmed Qazi, Zahoor Mistry, Shakir; code names Chief, Doctor,
  Burger, Bhola, Shankar).
- Amritsar: landed 18:44, left 19:47 — hijackers, upset by the refuelling delay, stabbed passengers Satnam
  Singh and Rupin Katyal; ordered Capt. Devi Sharan to take off; the plane narrowly avoided a fuel tanker.
- Lahore: landed 20:01, left ~22:32. Dubai (Al Minhad air base): landed 01:32 on 25 Dec; 27 passengers
  released; body of Rupin Katyal, 25, offloaded. Kandahar (Taliban): landed 08:33 on 25 Dec.
- Demands: release of 36 prisoners, the body of HuM founder Sajjad Afghani, US$200 million; narrowed to
  three: Masood Azhar, Ahmed Omar Saeed Sheikh, Mushtaq Ahmed Zargar.
- 31 Dec 1999: crisis ended; passengers and crew released. External Affairs Minister Jaswant Singh flew to
  Kandahar with the three prisoners. The Taliban gave the hijackers ten hours to leave and drove them to the
  Durand Line. 1 killed (Katyal), 17 injured.
- Later: CBI charged ten people; two convicted (life) in Feb 2008. Hijacker Zahoor Mistry shot dead in
  Karachi, 1 Mar 2022.

## The three
- **Masood Azhar** — arrested in India 1994; founded Jaish-e-Mohammed in 2000. JeM blamed (with LeT) for the
  13 Dec 2001 Parliament attack; linked to Pathankot (Jan 2016); claimed Pulwama (14 Feb 2019, 40 CRPF
  personnel killed). UN Security Council listing May 2019.
- **Ahmed Omar Saeed Sheikh** — British-born, attended LSE; arrested in India 1994 over the kidnapping of
  Western tourists meant to free jailed militants. After Kandahar, in Pakistan. Wall Street Journal reporter
  Daniel Pearl kidnapped in Karachi, Jan 2002, and killed. Sheikh convicted by a Pakistani anti-terrorism
  court in 2002 (death sentence); Sindh High Court overturned the murder conviction in 2020; Pakistan's
  Supreme Court acquitted him in Jan 2021; moved to a government "safe house" under guard (CPJ, Al Jazeera,
  Inquirer/AP).
- **Mushtaq Ahmed Zargar ("Latram")** — from Nowhatta, Srinagar; founder of Al-Umar Mujahideen; jailed in
  India from 1992. After release based in Pakistan and Illegally Occupied Pakistani Kashmir. Designated a
  terrorist under the UAPA by the Union Home Ministry, 13 Apr 2022: "running an incessant campaign from
  Pakistan to fuel militancy in Jammu and Kashmir" (Kashmir Observer, Greater Kashmir, Tribune India).
