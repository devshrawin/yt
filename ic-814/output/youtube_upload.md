## Description (4798/5000)
```
On December 31, 1999, India's foreign minister flew to a Taliban-held runway in Kandahar with three prisoners, to end the seven-day hijacking of Indian Airlines Flight 814. File 06 of The Sisodia Files follows what those three men did next: Masood Azhar founded Jaish-e-Mohammed; Omar Sheikh was convicted in Pakistan over the kidnapping of Daniel Pearl; Mushtaq Zargar kept recruiting for militancy in Kashmir — and the chain that ran from Kandahar to the 2001 standoff and to Pulwama and Balakot.

Built from the public record of the hijacking, Pakistani court rulings in the Pearl case, India's UAPA designation of Zargar, the UN Security Council listing of Azhar, and reporting by the Committee to Protect Journalists, Al Jazeera and Tribune India. Contested claims are labelled in the narration.
```
