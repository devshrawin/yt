## Description (5208/5000)
```
In November 2008, a Chicago doctor checked into the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel in Mumbai — about eleven days before gunmen walked its corridors. File 03 of The Sisodia Files follows Tahawwur Rana: the boarding-school friendship with David Coleman Headley, the immigration business that became Headley's cover, the 2011 Chicago verdict that acquitted him of the Mumbai conspiracy while convicting him of supporting Lashkar-e-Taiba, and the legal gap that let India extradite him in April 2025.

Built from court filings, the NIA's case, US Justice Department and State Department statements, and PBS Frontline's reporting. The case is ongoing: status is current to October 2025 reporting, and no verdict had been reached.
```
