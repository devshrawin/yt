## Who he is
Tahawwur Hussain Rana. Born January 12, 1961, Chichawatni, Sahiwal District, Punjab, Pakistan.
Father a retired school principal from Lahore; one brother a psychiatrist in the Pakistan
military, another a journalist for Canada's *The Hill Times*. Pakistani-Canadian, later a
Chicago resident. 64 at extradition (April 2025).

## Hasan Abdal
Rana and David Coleman Headley (born Daood Sayed Gilani) met as boys at Cadet College Hasan
Abdal, an elite military prep school in Punjab ("Abdalians"). Headley — American mother,
Pakistani father, noted for heterochromia. Close for decades. Rana's wife Samraz told PBS
Frontline the family considered Headley family: he called her "sister", her children called
him "special uncle". The bond survived Rana's emigration to Canada (1997) and the US, and
Headley's path through drug trafficking, a DEA-informant stint and radicalisation.

## Career
Medical school in Pakistan; Captain / General Duty Practitioner, Pakistan Army Medical Corps.
Deserted in the late 1990s; emigrated to Canada with his wife (also a physician) in 1997; both
Canadian citizens June 2001. Moved to Chicago: a halal meat business on Devon Avenue, and First
World Immigration Services (offices in Chicago, New York, Toronto; later a Mumbai branch).

## The Mumbai front
Headley used the Mumbai branch as cover for repeated reconnaissance trips for LeT before the
2008 attack. Rana travelled to India ~11 days before the attack and stayed at the Taj Mahal
Palace Hotel. NIA records place him in Agra, Kochi and Hapur under the same consultant cover.
Maharashtra ATS says recordings exist of Rana and Headley congratulating LeT leadership on the
"excellent planning and execution" of the attack.

## Sajid Mir
Early 2009: Headley called Rana in Chicago to play him an audio recording of at least five
senior LeT commanders giving real-time instructions to the ten gunmen. One voice impressed
Rana: Sajid Majid, alias Sajid Mir, a still-absconding alleged mastermind.

## Denmark plot and arrest
Plot against Jyllands-Posten (Prophet Muhammad cartoons, 2005); reportedly included beheading
employees. Both men arrested by US federal agents at O'Hare Airport, Chicago, October 18, 2009.

## 2011 trial
Headley pleaded guilty March 2010, testified against Rana; 35 years and, under his plea deal,
protection from extradition to India. Rana's trial: May 16 – June 9, 2011, Chicago federal
court. Acquitted: conspiracy to provide material support to the Mumbai attacks. Convicted:
conspiracy to support the Denmark plot; material support to LeT. 14 years + 5 years supervised
release (Judge Harry Leinenweber, who called it "a dastardly plot"). Defence: manipulated;
believed assignments came from Pakistan's ISI. Prosecution: emails, wiretaps, post-arrest
statements.

## Why extradition was possible
India's charges (murder, waging war, forgery, terrorism under Indian law) were never tried in
the US; no double jeopardy. Ninth Circuit: the India-charged crimes "have elements independent
from those under which [Rana] was prosecuted in the United States."

## Extradition timeline
2018 NIA warrant · 2020 released early (COVID), re-arrested days later on India's request ·
June 2020 formal request · May 16, 2023 magistrate certifies · Aug 10, 2023 habeas denied ·
Aug 15, 2024 Ninth Circuit affirms · Jan 21, 2025 cert denied · Mar 6, 2025 Justice Kagan
rejects stay · Apr 4, 2025 final petition dismissed (incl. UN Convention Against Torture claim)
· Apr 9–10, 2025 transferred from Los Angeles, landed Delhi, arrested by NIA on arrival.
US Secretary of State Marco Rubio: Rana would "face justice" for his role.

## Since arrival (to October 2025)
Tihar Jail; questioned 8–10 hours a day early on ("evasive answers"); voice and handwriting
samples taken to match intercepted Headley communications; Solicitor General Tushar Mehta
leads prosecution; supplementary chargesheet July 2025; as of October 2025 India still
requesting details from the US. **No trial verdict as of this research — say so on screen.**
