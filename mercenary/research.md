## PART 1 — BACKGROUND RESEARCH

### Who he is
Born **Daood Sayed Gilani** on June 30, 1960, in Washington, D.C. His father, Sayed Salim Gilani, was a Pakistani broadcaster/diplomat; his mother, Alice Serrill Headley, was an American from a wealthy Philadelphia family. He was raised in Pakistan from infancy, attended the elite **Cadet College Hasan Abdal** (where he met Tahawwur Rana), and returned to the US at 17 to live with his mother in Philadelphia. He had heterochromia (one green eye, one brown), spoke Urdu and English, and reportedly claimed some Pashto, Farsi and Arabic. He changed his name to **David Coleman Headley** (using his mother's maiden name) — legally in 1977 per some accounts, and deliberately used the Western name for his India missions from 2006.

### The drug years and the DEA
- **1988** — arrested in Frankfurt, Germany, carrying two kilos of heroin from Pakistan. He cooperated, gave up partners, and served about four years while his co-defendant got ten.
- Ran video-rental stores in Manhattan (bought by his family) after release.
- **1997** — arrested again in a DEA sting in a Manhattan hotel over another heroin deal. He signed on as a **DEA confidential informant**, even travelling to Pakistan for the agency, and served roughly 15 months instead of a possible nine-year sentence.
- After 9/11 the DEA reportedly had him collect intelligence on extremists too. The DEA says its last contract with him ended **March 27, 2002**; Headley says he stopped in September 2002.

### The turn to Lashkar
In 2002, while still an informant, Headley attended an LeT event in Pakistan, was moved by a lecture from Hafiz Saeed, and joined. Per the US Department of Justice, he attended **five LeT training courses between 2002 and 2005** — the first, a basic *daura* in Muzaffarabad, reportedly overseen by Zaki-ur-Rehman Lakhvi, with later training in the Mansehra area. This connects directly to the training structure in your first video. In late 2005 he received instructions from three LeT members to travel to India and conduct surveillance.

A US counter-terror official quoted by NBC/ProPublica gave the sharpest one-line read of him: he saw Headley "as a mercenary, not ideologically driven."

### The Mumbai mission (2006–2008)
- Changed his name officially for cover; LeT handler **Sajid Mir** was discussing sending him to India by 2005.
- With money from an ISI-linked handler (Major Iqbal, per Indian reporting), he opened the **Mumbai branch of Rana's First World Immigration Services** as his front.
- Made **five surveillance trips to Mumbai (2006–2008)**, filming and GPS-marking targets: the Taj Mahal Palace, Oberoi-Trident, Leopold Café, Nariman House, CST and more. He stayed at the Taj and Oberoi-Trident, sometimes with his Moroccan wife Faiza Outalha. He also befriended **Rahul Bhatt**, son of filmmaker Mahesh Bhatt, who showed him around Mumbai gyms and social circles.
- In court he later said LeT had also considered other targets, including the Siddhivinayak Temple and a defence-scientists' conference at the Taj, and that two earlier sea-borne attempts in **September and October 2008** failed because of accidents.
- After 26/11 he made a further scouting trip in 2009 (including Delhi's National Defence College) and, per his own testimony, worked with **Ilyas Kashmiri's** al-Qaeda-linked 313 Brigade on further plots. (Kashmiri gave him money for airfare, he said.)

### The Denmark plot
In 2009 Headley did a similar scouting mission in **Copenhagen** (and Aarhus) against Danish newspaper *Jyllands-Posten*, which had published the Muhammad cartoons. He was arrested before it was carried out.

### The warnings that were missed
This is the most important accountability thread in the story. A ProPublica/Washington Post review led by the Director of National Intelligence found **at least five separate warnings** about Headley:
1. **2001 and 2002** — tips about extremist sympathies (including after he went to Pakistan as an informant).
2. **2005** — his American wife told FBI agents in New York (after a domestic dispute) that he was an active LeT militant who had trained in its camps. The FBI "looked into" it; nothing came of it.
3. **2007** — his Moroccan wife, Faiza Outalha, met US Embassy officials in Islamabad twice and showed photos of their stays at the Taj and Oberoi. Her line, as reported: "he's either a terrorist, or he's working for you."
4. **April 2008** — another tip surfaced.
5. **December 2008** — a further tip arrived a month *after* the attack.
US officials' position: the tips were too general to link him to a specific plot, and had they known specifics they'd have passed them to India. Headley wasn't caught until **11 months after** Mumbai, when British intelligence flagged his contact with al-Qaeda figures in Europe.

### Arrest and plea
- **Aug 5, 2009** — returned via Atlanta and lied to a CBP inspector about his trip.
- **Oct 3, 2009** — arrested at **O'Hare Airport, Chicago**, about to fly to Pakistan to deliver roughly 13 surveillance videos to his handlers.
- **March 2010** — pleaded guilty to all 12 counts and began cooperating (the plea deal took the death penalty off the table and promised no extradition to India).
- **May–June 2011** — testified against Rana in Chicago.
- **Jan 24, 2013** — sentenced by Judge Harry Leinenweber to **35 years** plus five years' supervised release. The judge said: "Mr. Headley is a terrorist," and the government argued life imprisonment would otherwise have been appropriate "but for" his cooperation.

### The 2015–16 Indian testimony
- **Dec 10, 2015** — appeared by video link from an undisclosed US location before Judge G.A. Sanap in Mumbai, agreed to turn approver, and was **pardoned** by the Indian court (the pardon doesn't affect his US sentence).
- **Feb 8–13, 2016** — examination by Special Public Prosecutor Ujjwal Nikam; **Mar 23–26, 2016** — cross-examination by Abu Jundal's defence lawyer. It was the first time a foreign terrorist testified before an Indian court.
- Key claims: named ISI officers ("Major Iqbal", "Mr Ali", Brigadier Riyaz) as involved with LeT handlers; said the ISI supported multiple groups; named **Ishrat Jahan** as an LeT operative (a hearsay-based claim that became politically contested in India).

### Since then
Reports in July 2018 said Headley was attacked by fellow inmates in a US prison; his condition afterwards is not publicly documented. India has repeatedly sought access to him; the US said in 2013 it would not allow further access, though NIA had reportedly discussed questioning him again after Rana's 2025 extradition. He remains in US custody, undisclosed location.

### Caveats to state in the video
- Headley's own testimony is the source for many claims, and he is a self-admitted liar, trafficker and informant. Present his claims as "testified", not "proven".
- The exact number of Mumbai trips is reported as five (some Indian reports say six including one post-attack trip).
- Whether US agencies "used" him deliberately is speculation; the documented failure is missed warnings, not proven collusion.
