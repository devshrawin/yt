## Description (6108/5000)
```
In October 2009, FBI agents stopped a man at Chicago's O'Hare Airport as he was about to fly to Pakistan with surveillance videos for his handlers. File 04 of The Sisodia Files follows David Coleman Headley: the heroin arrests that turned him into a DEA informant, the Lashkar-e-Taiba training, the five scouting trips that mapped the 26/11 targets, the at least five warnings US authorities received before the attack, and the plea deal that kept him out of India's reach.

Built from Headley's guilty plea and sentencing record, US Justice Department releases, the Director of National Intelligence review reported by ProPublica and The Washington Post, PBS Frontline, and reporting on his 2016 testimony to a Mumbai court. Claims that rest on Headley's own testimony are labelled as such.
```
