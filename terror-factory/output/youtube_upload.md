Using court testimony from Ajmal Kasab and David Coleman Headley, research from West Point's Combating Terrorism Center and the Counter Extremism Project, and India's Operation Sindoor briefing, File 01 of The Sisodia Files reconstructs the LeT training pipeline stage by stage — from Muridke to Muzaffarabad, from Daura-e-Sufa to Daura-e-Khaas — and the May 2025 strikes on nine camps.

Every claim in this video is sourced. Allegations are labelled as allegations.
